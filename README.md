### 👋 About me

I'm a mechatronics engineering student at **ENISo** (Sousse, Tunisia), graduating in 2027. I build things that sense, decide and move: **STM32 firmware**, **IoT networks** and **ROS 2 robots**.

- 🔭 **Currently building:** a multi-robot ROS 2 / Gazebo system covering simulation, navigation and LLM-driven mission planning
- 🎯 **Looking for:** a **6-month engineering internship from February 2027** in embedded systems or robotics
- 🌱 **Learning:** data science and AI fundamentals (GCI World 2026 program) and MISRA-compliant C
- 🤝 **Open to collaborate on:** open-source robotics and embedded projects: ROS 2 packages, STM32 firmware, simulation tools
- 💬 **Ask me about:** STM32, FreeRTOS, CAN, UART, SPI, I2C, ROS 2, Gazebo, PCB design with Altium
- ⚡ **Fun fact:** I talk to microcontrollers more than I should. They answer in hex.

### 🚀 Featured projects

- 🤖 **LLM-coordinated logistics robots**: 4 lift-under mobile robots in ROS 2 / Gazebo, multi-agent path planning and real-time conflict resolution, with Gemini turning natural-language requests into missions. *ROS 2 · Python · C++*
- 👁️ **Vision-based obstacle detection & tracking** *(in progress)*: real-time detection of static and moving obstacles with OpenCV and YOLO, avoidance with fuzzy logic + PID. *ROS 2 · OpenCV · YOLO*
- ⚙️ **Speed synchronization of two brushless motors**: field-oriented control (FOC) on STSPIN32F0 with a real-time FreeRTOS control loop, speed mismatch kept under 2 % (with ACTIA Engineering). *C · FreeRTOS*
- 🏭 **Industrial production monitoring firmware (SOPAL)**: robust ESP32-C6 firmware with queue management and secured production data. *C · ESP32-C6*
- 🏠 **[Connected home automation system](https://github.com/AmineNsiri001/CSF_internship_project)**: lights, blinds and A/C controlled from a phone, with an STM32F407 + ESP8266, MQTT, Node-RED and a Flutter app. *STM32 · MQTT · Flutter*
